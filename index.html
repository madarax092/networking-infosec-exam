How scoring works

Each question is worth 5 points for a correct final answer (0 if wrong or blank). On top of that, how you handle each question can add a penalty:

Marked For Review, left blank0
Marked For Review, answered, never changed0
Marked For Review, answered, changed N times−N
Not flagged, Checked left unticked−2
Not flagged, Checked ticked, left blank−5
Not flagged, Checked ticked, answered, never changed0
Not flagged, Checked ticked, answered, changed N times−N

50 questions × 5 points = 250 possible. 175 points (70%) is a pass. Nothing is scored until you press Submit on the summary page — you can revisit and change any question before then.
